CARDANO-NODE — documented ChainDB behaviour (ouroboros-consensus)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Storage: ImmutableDB (finalized, > k) + VolatileDB (recent, < k) + LedgerDB
  (on-disk ledger-state snapshots + in-memory). The stake distribution (Shelley
  "mark/set/go" snapshots) is PART OF the ledger state itself -> self-contained.
Startup: ChainDB opens, validates, and REPLAYS blocks from the most recent usable
  ledger snapshot (falling back to an older snapshot, or genesis) to reconstruct the
  volatile/ledger state; VolatileDB is scanned and corrupt/truncated tails dropped.
  => self-healing recovery; no "missing N-2 stake distribution" is possible because a
     reload reconstructs the full self-consistent ledger state, and from-genesis replay
     is always available as the ultimate fallback.
