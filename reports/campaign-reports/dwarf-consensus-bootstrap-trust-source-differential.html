DWARF — Bootstrap Trust-Source Differential

"Where did this node's truth come from?" · cardano-node vs Amaru · source analysis · 2026-07-18
Two nodes can reach the same chain tip while establishing their initial trusted state by completely different means. DWARF's oracles watch tip convergence; they don't model trust provenance. Result: cardano-node can bootstrap trustlessly from genesis; Amaru structurally requires a trusted snapshot and carries compiled-in trust anchors — including the consensus-critical leader-election nonces. The two clients do not share a root of trust for their initial state. No exploit — a model gap DWARF should test.

The load-bearing anchor — Amaru's initial nonces

Amaru embeds the initial Ouroboros nonces into the binary, per network — these seed leader election:

cardano-node derives these by validating history from a genesis it can check. A wrong or tampered nonces.json would give Amaru a different leader schedule than cardano-node — a real consensus divergence originating entirely in a build-time trust anchor, and one a tip-convergence oracle would only notice after the fact.

Why it matters (no exploit — a model gap)

Pinned nonces + Mithril snapshots are a legitimate, audited fast-bootstrap design (cardano-node uses Mithril too). The finding is that the two nodes' trust roots differ, and DWARF's convergence oracles can't detect a divergence caused by a bad trust source. Amaru's bootstrap security depends on its compiled-in anchors (build/supply-chain trust) and the imported snapshot (Mithril integrity, or the snapshot producer); cardano-node's from-genesis mode depends on neither, and it has a checkpoint defence Amaru lacks.

What DWARF should add

A trust-source dimension to the differential harness: bootstrap each node from each supported source (genesis / checkpoint / Mithril / synthetic snapshot), record the provenance of the initial state, and assert the two nodes anchored on compatible roots — not just that their tips converged. Tampering a bootstrap anchor (a nonce, a snapshot) and checking whether the node detects it is the natural adversarial follow-up. Source citations in consensus-bootstrap-trust-source-evidence/.
